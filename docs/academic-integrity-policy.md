# KOBO Academic Integrity Policy

Effective for the KOBO MVP pilot.

KOBO allows academic support that helps a learner understand, improve, or present their own work. Permitted services include tutoring, concept explanations, proofreading, formatting, research guidance, study planning, and feedback on a learner's draft.

KOBO does not allow a provider to:

- complete an examination, test, quiz, or other assessment for someone else;
- impersonate a student or access a learner's institution account;
- write or complete graded work that a learner will submit as their own;
- obtain, sell, or distribute leaked assessment materials;
- falsify research, results, attendance, references, or academic records; or
- advertise a service whose purpose is to bypass an institution's academic rules.

Listings, services, requests, offers, and messages that violate this policy may be removed. Accounts may be restricted or suspended. KOBO may preserve relevant records when required to investigate a report, protect users, or meet legal obligations.

Users should describe the legitimate learning outcome they need. Providers should keep the learner involved, distinguish guidance from authorship, and refuse work that would misrepresent who completed an assessment.

Reports can be submitted through KOBO's reporting tools. This MVP policy is product guidance and must be reviewed with UCC and Ghana-specific legal counsel before launch.
